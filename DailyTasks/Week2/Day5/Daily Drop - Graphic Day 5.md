## Section B: The Execution Mandate (9:00 AM - 2:00 PM)

### 1. Execution Summary (TL;DR)
Objective: Package your Week 2 design work into a professional portfolio case study. Add micro-interaction animations using Figma Smart Animate. Present your prototype in a structured 5-minute demo format.

### 2. The Workflow:

9:00 AM - 10:00 AM (Learning): Watch all three videos. Study 2 Behance case studies in your domain (dashboard design). Note their structure: Problem → Research → Ideation → Design → Iteration → Result. This is the format you will follow today.

10:00 AM - 1:00 PM (Building):
Action 1 (Case Study Slides): Create a new Figma page "Case Study — Week 2". Build a 10-12 frame presentation covering: Problem Statement (what user problem does your design solve?), Design Process (wireframe → hi-fi progression), Key Design Decisions (3 decisions with rationale), Usability Findings (from Day 4 heuristic evaluation), Before/After Iterations, and Final Design Showcase.
Action 2 (Micro-Interactions): Add Smart Animate transitions to your prototype: button press state (scale 0.95 on click), card hover (subtle elevation shadow), modal open/close (fade + slide), and navigation item active state. Test all transitions in Presentation mode.
Action 3 (Export & Portfolio): Export the case study as a PDF presentation (File > Export as PDF). Record a 5-minute Loom screen recording of you presenting through the Figma prototype. Write a brief project summary (150 words) suitable for a Behance or LinkedIn portfolio post.

The Non-Negotiable Rules:
1. Case study must explain design DECISIONS and RATIONALE — not just "here is what I made."
2. All micro-interactions must use Smart Animate — static frame transitions are not acceptable.
3. Your Loom recording must show the prototype running in Figma Presentation mode, not the editor.

1:00 PM - 1:30 PM (Hygiene): Final Figma review: all layers named, all components from Design System, accessibility checked with Stark plugin. Export final screens at 2x. Organise your GitHub /designs/ folder.

1:30 PM - 2:00 PM (LinkedIn): "Week 2 Design complete at ProSensia. Went from wireframe → design system → hi-fi → prototype → case study → Loom demo. Design without rationale is just decoration. Design with rationale is a professional deliverable."
